# Иллюстрации курса

Созданы встроенным image_gen 2026-09-09. Референс идентичности: `frontend/assets/imgs/citavuk_rule.webp`.
Финальные общие ассеты: `frontend/assets/course/citavuk-{guide,reading,celebrate}-v1.webp`.
React получает их штатным `prepare-course-assets.mjs`; Flutter загружает встроенные WebP без сети.
Упаковка уменьшает разрешение до 720 px с сохранением альфы; рисунок не перерисовывается скриптом.

## guide

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent-background mascot illustration for the Serbian grammar course in Citavuk, shared React and Flutter. Image 1 is an IDENTITY and ART STYLE REFERENCE, not an image to reproduce. Preserve this exact recognizable grey-brown wolf Citavuk: friendly expressive large eyes, cream muzzle, pink ear interiors, charcoal Serbian cap with red trim and gold emblem, embroidered white shirt, black vest with red/gold embroidery, red sash, dark trousers, fluffy tail. Match the warm hand-painted linework, NOT a flat green owl, NOT a stiff articulated puppet. Full character comfortably framed, paws and ears completely inside image, compact expressive silhouette suitable for display 180–300px. Genuine transparent background with clean alpha, no white sticker outline or halos, no rectangular backdrop, no scenery, no lettering, no UI, no watermark. Pose: welcoming teacher standing in an energetic three-quarter pose, holding one open red-bound grammar book in one paw, other paw warmly pointing toward a nearby lesson path, delighted confident expression. Small grounded soft shadow directly beneath feet only. Rich but restrained embroidered details.
